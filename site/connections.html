How to play

Find groups of four dictionary words that share something in common.

  • Pick four words and tap Submit to check them.
  • Each group is one category from the wiki.
  • Each word belongs to only one group on the board.
  • You can make four mistakes before the game ends.

Category colors go from straightforward to tricky:

Yellow: the easiest
Green
Blue
Purple: the trickiest

A new puzzle appears every day at midnight, and everyone gets the same one.
