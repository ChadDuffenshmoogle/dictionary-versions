Next Predicted Word ?Made-up guess from the spelling patterns in the entries. The tool counts which letter usually follows each group of letters (for example, what often comes after "tio"), then builds a new word one letter at a time by picking likely next letters. It only keeps words that are not already in the dictionary or too close to one, and the guess changes only when a new word is added. The definition is built the same way, one word at a time, from which words usually follow each group of words, and it may not copy an existing definition. Normal looks 4 letters back for the word and 2 words back for the definition. Nonsense looks only 2 letters and 1 word back, so the result is looser and stranger.
